Undergraduate Thesis

Rick Steves and the Making of the Middle-Class Tourist

How Rick Steves Redefined Travel as a Middle-Class Identity and the Structural Barriers That Persist

This thesis explores how Rick Steves uses television, guidebooks, and digital media to teach Americans how to travel, interpret Europe, and perform a specific middle-class tourist identity. It argues that while Steves expands cultural access to travel, structural barriers such as money, time, passport privilege, and mobility still remain.

About me

Hey, I’m Quade — a writer passionate about telling stories that reveal something real, whether it’s about digital media, street basketball in Prague, travel culture, or late-night rooftop drinks in Istanbul. I’ve studied journalism and media, and I use my writing to explore identity, culture, and connection in unexpected places.

In every piece I write, I try to keep it authentic. That means emotion, context, and a clear point of view.

I believe writing isn’t just about what you say — it’s how you make people feel when they read it.
